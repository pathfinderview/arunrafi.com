A LITTLE NOTE FOR GROWN-UPS

A small habit.
A whole world.

Five minutes together is a lovely place to start. Let your child guess aloud, reveal the name, and notice the details together.

The six-month path

  1. Months 1–3 · DiscoverTwo new flags a day, plus up to six familiar flags due for another look.
  2. Month 4 · Complete the collectionAt this pace, all 195 arrive in about 14 weeks. Take longer whenever you need.
  3. Months 5–6 · Make them stickRevisit up to eight familiar flags a day. Describe a detail, then try recalling the country before revealing it.

“I knew it” means your child named the country before the reveal. We revisit after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days. Six successful recalls on due days earns “Remembered”. “Still learning” brings it back tomorrow. No penalties, no lost streaks.

Progress stays in this browser, on this device. Save a backup to move it to another device. The six-month path is a practice plan, not a promise—follow your child’s pace.

About the flags and audio

193 UN members, plus Palestine and Vatican City. Flag artwork from Flagpedia / FlagCDN; country names and codes adapted from mledoze/countries under the ODbL. Afghanistan shows the tricolour variant; other variants are in use. Paraguay shows the front face. Artwork checked November 2026.

Recorded names and descriptions play when you reveal or tap. Keep device sound on. The sound button mutes narration. No microphone or recording is used.
