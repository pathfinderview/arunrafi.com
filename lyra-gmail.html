Lyra Personal Gmail

Privacy and use information · Updated 16 November 2026

Lyra Personal Gmail is Arun Rafi’s personal Gmail integration for his own OpenClaw assistant. It is not a public service and does not offer public registration.

Privacy

The integration requests read-only Gmail access and Google account identity information. It reads authorized email to help with personal tasks, including identifying payment alerts and reconciling expenses.

Selected email evidence and derived records may be stored in the owner’s private assistant workspace and processed by the configured assistant/model services to perform those tasks. Email contents and expense records are not published on this website.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The account owner can revoke access through Google Account permissions and delete saved tokens and records from the private workspace. Revoking authorization stops future API access but does not itself delete previously saved records.

Terms of use

This integration is for the account owner’s personal use only. It is provided as-is, without a guarantee of continuous availability or accuracy. Financial summaries require source verification and are not a banking service.

For questions about the integration or data deletion, contact hi@arunrafi.com.
