I'm writing here again.

The latest essay is The Shape of Mastery. It began with a thought: understanding the limitations of a system can make it possible to do more with it.

I'm interested in the connection between learning something and knowing where it breaks. Alongside that, I keep returning to effort, judgement, and what happens when work that used to be difficult becomes easy.

This site is where I work through those questions, one short essay at a time.
