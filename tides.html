About this model

The Moon is drawn ten times too close, and the bulges are drawn thousands of times too tall. On the open sea the real bulge is under a metre. Coasts and bays funnel it into the tides you see on a beach.

Earth's axis is drawn upright, and the Moon orbits over the equator. The arrows show the pull on each piece of the Earth. The beach is Marina Beach, Chennai.
