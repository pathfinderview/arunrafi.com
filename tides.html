About these pictures

You are looking down on the North Pole. The Moon is drawn ten to twenty times too close, and the bulges thousands of times too tall: on the open sea the real bulge is under a metre, and coasts funnel it into the tides you see. The Moon's orbit is drawn over the equator. The beach is Marina Beach, Chennai. "Today" uses the real phase of the Moon.
